Privacy Policy: Everyday Gym

This is a translation of the Portuguese version, which prevails in case of conflict.

Version: 1 · Effective: November 16, 2026

Summary

  • Your data stays on your phone. Everyday Gym has no server, and the developer does not receive, see or keep any of your data.
  • The app has no internet access. It does not have Android's internet permission, and you can check this on the app's Play Store page and in your phone's settings.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Screenshots of your workout are read on the phone itself. The image is not sent anywhere.
  • You control the backup. A backup is a file you save wherever you like, and you can delete everything at any time.

1. Who we are

Everyday Gym is a free app developed by Renan de Castan e Carvalho, an individual developer in Brazil ("we"). Privacy contact: renancastan@live.com.

Because the app sends no data to us, we do not process your personal data: it is stored and used only on your device, under your control.

2. What data the app keeps

The data below is kept only in the app's private storage, on your device:

Data Purpose
Your name or nickname (optional) Greeting you on the home screen
Training plans, exercises, sets, reps and weights Building and following your workouts
Workout history and weights lifted Counters, remembered weights, suggestions and charts
Machine settings and names learned during import Making the next workouts and imports easier
Custom exercises, with the photos and videos you add Showing how the exercise is done
Workout screenshots you import Read on the device to create the plan; they are not stored
Body weight and measurements (optional) Tracking your physical progress
Preferences (reminders, alerts, language, theme) Setting up the app

Body weight and measurements are sensitive personal data (health) under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018). They never leave your device, except inside a backup that you decide to save (section 4).

3. What the app does NOT do

  • It does not access the internet.
  • It does not collect usage data, location, contacts or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not use ad, analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs. Google's text recognition library (ML Kit) runs entirely on the device, and the network permission it would request has been removed from the app.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.

4. Backup

  • A backup is a .zip file with your data (including your preferences and the name you entered) and the photos and videos of your custom exercises. You choose where to save it with Android's file picker: on the phone, on a computer or in a cloud service that shows up in the picker, such as Google Drive.
  • The app does not sign in to any account and only accesses the file you choose. If you save it to a cloud service, the file is subject to that service's rules.
  • The backup contains sensitive data (section 2) and is not encrypted. Keep it somewhere safe.
  • Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for the app.

5. Android permissions

Permission When Why
Notifications Only when you turn on an alert Workout reminders, plan expiry and end of rest
Vibration Always (no prompt) Signaling the end of rest
Run at startup and in the background Always (no prompt) Rescheduling reminders after the phone restarts and delivering them on time

Access to photos and videos uses the system picker: the app only receives the files you choose or share with it.

6. Google Play and Android

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about installs and, if you allowed it in your phone's settings, Android crash reports. Google does this, not the app, under Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The developer only receives aggregated statistics from Google, which do not identify you.

7. Your rights (LGPD)

Because the data stays on your device, you exercise your rights directly in the app:

  • Access and portability: Settings → "Back up" (a file with your data in JSON and your exercise media).
  • Correction: edit your data in the app's screens.
  • Deletion: Settings → "Delete all data", or uninstall the app, which removes everything from the device.
  • Questions: write to renancastan@live.com. You can also contact Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and shown in the app before it takes effect.
